Burroughs Landscaping is a locally owned crew serving Kerrville and the surrounding Hill Country.

Our approach

Mike Burroughs founded the company on a simple idea: landscape design should be intentional, balanced and personal — not a template dropped onto a lot. Every project starts with understanding your goals, your space and how you actually want to live outdoors, then a custom plan that ties it all together.

The Hill Country doesn't make that easy. Rocky terrain and thin soil mean a plan that works two counties over won't necessarily work here — so we design around the land instead of fighting it, choosing stone, plants and drainage paths that suit the property they're on.

From first sketch through construction and, for clients who want it, ongoing maintenance, it's the same crew the whole way — no handoffs, no lost details.
